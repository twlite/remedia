## FFmpeg 9.0.2 — statically linked

The native addon and server runtime include FFmpeg revision
946fcce07b6dcd0331c8cc609192aeff5e1924f8, configured with GPL and version3.
Combined binaries are distributed under GPLv3-or-later; see LICENSES.md and
COPYING.GPLv3. Corresponding source retrieval is described in SOURCE_DISTRIBUTION.md.
Linux, Android and FreeBSD TLS uses OpenSSL 3.6.0 (Apache-2.0); macOS uses system Secure Transport;
Windows uses system Schannel. No nonfree components are permitted.

## libopus — statically included

`opus2` 0.4.0 uses `libopus_sys` 0.3.3 with the bundled libopus backend. Its Opus source is compiled
and statically linked into the native addon and standalone server. A system libopus is not needed.
Its `opus/COPYING` and `opus/LICENSE_PLEASE_READ.txt`, including the applicable
copyright and patent notices, are reproduced in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` and
retained in the dependency source snapshot.

## Discord voice and cryptography

[DAVE (`davey` 0.2.0)](https://crates.io/crates/davey/0.2.0) and
[OpenMLS 0.8.3](https://crates.io/crates/openmls/0.8.3) provide native voice encryption components
under their upstream MIT terms. Native TLS/crypto also incorporates
[ring 0.17.14](https://crates.io/crates/ring/0.17.14), whose Apache-2.0 and ISC notices include
BoringSSL-derived code. Their actual license and copyright notices are in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.
