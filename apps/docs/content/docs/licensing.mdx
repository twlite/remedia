## Remedia and dependency notices

The [MIT license](https://github.com/twlite/remedia/blob/main/LICENSE) applies to Remedia.
Third-party dependencies retain their own terms. Distributed npm packages and server archives
include `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` plus the `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` appendix. The
[notices](https://github.com/twlite/remedia/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) reproduce dependency
copyright, license conditions, and disclaimers, including the bundled libopus backend.

## FFmpeg builds

FFmpeg 9 is dynamically linked. npm packages and standalone release archives require a separate FFmpeg installation. The Docker image provisions FFmpeg shared libraries during its build.
Its license depends on your chosen build, including GPL-enabled components and external libraries.
Remedia does not enforce LGPL-only builds. You must comply with applicable requirements for your
selected FFmpeg build and deployment. Consult the [official FFmpeg licensing information](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html)
and [source code](https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git).

## Covered source and redistribution

Some native dependencies use MPL-2.0. The notices identify exact published source versions. Preserve
the applicable notices and provide recipients access to the covered source as required by those
terms when redistributing. The presence of these files is not a guarantee of legal compliance.
See [package distribution](/docs/node/distribution) for FFmpeg ABI versions and runtime setup.
